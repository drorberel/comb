Who

Independent Consultant
Bio-Statistician · R Developer · RWE Researcher · HEOR

I am a statistical consultant with over 15 years of experience in academia, tech, and pharma. I bridge the gap between pre-clinical research and regulatory approval — ensuring robust and efficient trials and maximizing the value of pre-clinical data to inform decision-making for subsequent clinical phases.

I develop, review, and optimize analytical pipelines for complex scientific hypotheses, and I’m a one-stop shop for full-stack data science needs.

What I do

  • Clinical trial statistics — CDISC / ADaM, TLGs, regulatory submissions
  • R & Shiny development — automated pipelines, dashboards, REST APIs, Pharmaverse/TEAL tooling
  • Machine learning & causal inference — biomarker discovery, predictive modeling
  • Real-world evidence & HEOR — EHR and claims data, evidence-based decision support
